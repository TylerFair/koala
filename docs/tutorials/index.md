# Tutorials

Begin with a complete transit fit, then change one scientific assumption at a
time.

## Core workflow

- [Fit your first transit](soss_order1.md) follows a NIRISS/SOSS observation
  from configuration to spectrum.
- [Choose a systematics trend](../guides/trends.md) compares the visit shapes
  Koala can model.
- [Choose limb darkening](../guides/limb_darkening.md) explains the supported
  laws and priors.
- [Marginalize over models](../guides/model_stacking.md) carries uncertainty in
  those choices into the final spectrum.
